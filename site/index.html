Where the money goes

Four quiet leaks in every subscription product

dunning

Cards silently die

Failed renewals are 20-40% of all churn - and it is not even a decision. Nobody asked the user to update the card.

dead trials

Trials expire unpaid

Users sign up, poke around, run out of time - and nobody nudges them before the trial quietly ends.

silent cancels

Cancels nobody fights

Most users leave without a single save attempt - no pause offer, no downgrade, no win-back. Just gone.

missed upgrades

Power users stay cheap

Heavy users burn through plan limits and stay on the small plan - expansion revenue you never asked for.
