Questions

The questions people actually ask.

Can you guarantee recovered revenue?

No - and be careful with anyone who does: a revenue guarantee only pays off by blasting more volume, the exact thing that burns your brand. What we guarantee is delivery: your first campaign live within 7 days, or your first month is free. From there, every dollar we claim is measured against a control group you can inspect.

We already got burned by an agency.

The pattern that burns people is a lump sum for a black box and a report full of opens. We work the opposite way: dry-run until you flip the switch, every send logged, every claim checked against a holdout - and the leak audit shows the math behind every number on your screen.

Will you spam our users?

Frequency caps per person per day and per week, quiet hours in the user's own timezone, consent-first messaging, and a deterministic validator that blocks hype words, invented numbers and pushy copy before anything leaves. One unsubscribe is honored forever. We judge ourselves on replies and payments, never on send volume.

Whose name is on the emails?

Yours. Email from your domain, your sender, your signature; SMS and messengers under your brand; in-app banners inside your product. Users never see Retivo - and replies land where you read them.

What if our users speak different languages?

Each user gets the letter in their language - the engine decides per recipient from their locale, not per campaign. English and Russian today, more on request.

Who writes the copy?

Drafts are generated with your real business facts, then a rule engine - not a prompt - blocks cliches, unsourced numbers, shouting and repeated follow-ups. Subjects run as A/B pairs and the winner is fixed weekly on real clicks. You approve every text before it goes live.

What do you need from us to start?

A Stripe read-only key with a webhook and a one-line snippet on your site - or a read-only export from your product. No SDK, no engineering sprint, nothing to rip out later. Your data stays yours.

How do we start?

A free 30-minute teardown: bring your Stripe, we walk your funnel live and size the leak in dollars. If the leak is too small to matter, we tell you that too - and you keep the audit.
