# VIP Intelligence API Documentation

## Overview

VIP Intelligence is a comprehensive ML platform for VIP player management in iGaming. The platform provides predictive models covering VIP player identification, retention, and lifecycle management.

Built for iGaming operators and platform providers, predictions enable operators to take proactive actions, driving LTV and GGR growth when integrated into business processes.

The platform trains individual models using your historical data, then delivers daily predictions for integration into your systems.

***

## Quick Start

#### 1. Get API Credentials

Contact your account manager to receive your API credentials (`client_id` and `client_secret`). In the future, credentials will be available through your personal cabinet.

#### 2. Authenticate

Use your credentials to obtain an access token. This token authorizes all subsequent API requests and expires after 1 hour.

See Authentication section for details.

#### 3. Upload Historical Data

Provide your historical player data for model training:

**User data:** User profiles and VIP segment definitions

**Activity data:** Payments (deposits/withdrawals), bets, and daily balances

You can send either transaction-level data or daily aggregates depending on your data pipeline.

See Data Upload section for complete field specifications and examples.

#### 4. Wait for Model Training

Models are trained individually on your data to match your specific player behavior patterns. Player behavior varies significantly across operators, regions, and platforms—individual models deliver higher accuracy than generic ones.

You'll be notified when training completes and predictions are ready.

#### 5. Get Your First Prediction

Once models are ready, retrieve predictions via API. Three model types available:

* **Early VIP Detection** (`previp`) - Identify potential VIPs in their first 3-7 days
* **VIP Churn Prediction** (`vip-churn`) - Identify VIPs at risk of disengagement
* **VIP No-Growth Prediction** (`stable-vip`) - Identify VIPs unlikely to advance segments

See Models section for detailed examples and response formats.

#### 6. Daily Workflow

**Daily cycle:** Upload yesterday's activity data each morning → Retrieve fresh predictions the next day → Integrate into your CRM/BI systems.

Models generate predictions once daily. This matches retention team capacity and optimizes resource efficiency—teams can't act faster than daily anyway.

See Use Cases section for real-world integration examples.

## Authentication

Use your API credentials (api\_key and api\_secret) to obtain an access token. Include this token in the Authorization header for all API requests.

Use the access token in the `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header for all subsequent requests.

### Data Format Choice

**Detailed vs Aggregated:** For bets and payments, choose the format that matches your data pipeline - either individual transactions or daily summaries grouped by key dimensions.

Use detailed endpoints (`/v1/data/bets`, `/v1/data/payments`) when you have transaction-level data. Use daily aggregates (`/v1/data/bets-daily`) when you prefer to send pre-aggregated statistics.

### Aggregation Rules

When using daily aggregated endpoints, group your data by the following dimensions:

**bets-daily:**

* Group by: date, user\_id, device\_type, game\_id, currency
* Metrics: bet\_sum, bet\_count, win\_sum, win\_count

## Models (Reference)

### Early VIP Detection (previp)

Early VIP Detection identifies potential VIP players in their first 3-7 days of activity, before they reach VIP status through traditional observation periods.

The model analyzes player behavior patterns (deposits, betting activity, geography) during their first few days to predict who will achieve your defined VIP criteria within 30 days. Three progressive models (3-30, 5-30, 7-30) refine predictions as players accumulate more history.

**Why it matters:** Early identification enables proactive engagement when players are most receptive (days 3-7), accelerating VIP conversion and increasing player lifetime value. Traditional approaches wait 30+ days before taking action - by then, the optimal engagement window has passed.
